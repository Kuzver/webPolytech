Why Strength Training?

Strength training offers a multitude of benefits for women, including:

  • Increased muscle mass and tone
  • Improved bone density
  • Boosted metabolism
  • Better physical performance
  • Increased confidence

Beginner's Full Body Workout

This plan is designed for beginners and can be done 2-3 times a week.

Warm-up (5 minutes)

  • Light cardio (e.g., jogging in place, jumping jacks)
  • Dynamic stretches (e.g., arm circles, leg swings)

Workout (30-40 minutes)

  • Squats (3 sets of 10-12 reps)
  • Push-ups (3 sets of as many reps as possible, or on knees if needed)
  • Dumbbell Rows (3 sets of 10-12 reps per side)
  • Plank (3 sets, hold for 30-60 seconds)
  • Glute Bridges (3 sets of 12-15 reps)

Cool-down (5 minutes)

  • Static stretches (e.g., hamstring stretch, quadriceps stretch)
  • Deep breathing
